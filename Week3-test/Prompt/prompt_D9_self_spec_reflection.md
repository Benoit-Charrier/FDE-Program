# Prompt: Deliverable D9 — Self-Spec Build-Loop Reflection

## What this deliverable is

A one-page reflection on your own build loop. Written after completing D5B and D5 on your MedFlex D4 spec.

Output file: `Deliverables/D9_self_spec_reflection.md`

---

## Inputs

- `Deliverables/D5B_build_loop_analysis.md` — what was built and what questions the builder raised
- `Deliverables/D5_build_loop_response.md` — your classified signal inventory and spec revisions

---

## Required structure

### 1. What it built
What did the builder actually produce from your spec? Be specific — name the sections, components, or behaviours that came out correctly, and note anything that surprised you (built differently than you imagined, or not built at all).

### 2. What it asked
What questions did the builder raise before or during the build? For each question, one sentence on why the spec left that gap open.

### 3. What you'd change in the spec
Based on what was built and what was asked, what would you revise in D4? Name the specific section and the specific change — not "I would be clearer about X" but the actual revised intent.

---

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fits on one page
- [ ] §1 names specific outputs, not just "it built the agent"
- [ ] §2 traces each builder question back to a gap in the spec
- [ ] §3 names specific D4 sections with specific changes — no vague improvement language
