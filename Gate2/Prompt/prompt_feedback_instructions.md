### Minimum feedback bar (Gate 2 rubric focus)

Your returned feedback on each submission must open with an **executive summary** of three bullet points before any other section. Each bullet is one sentence. Cover in order:
1. The overall calibration — is this submission tracking toward a Gate 2 pass or not, and the single most important reason (name the specific deliverable element that drives the verdict)
2. The most critical gap — the one change that would most improve the submission's score if fixed before Friday (name the specific element: cognitive load map, delegation matrix, or agent purpose document)
3. The primary strength — what this participant does well that they must preserve and carry into the gate

This section must be self-contained — a participant who reads only this section should know where they stand, what to fix first, and what not to break.

It must also include a table of contents listing all sections by name, generated after the full document is written — titles must match exactly.

It must also include:

- **At least 3 specific gaps** named against specific elements of the cognitive load map, delegation matrix, or agent purpose document — each gap with a proposed minimum-change fix
- **A delegation-archetype calibration comment:** does the submission justify why each task cluster gets the delegation level it gets, or does it default everything to "fully agentic"? This is the Week 2 anti-pattern peer review must catch
- **A lived-work vs documented-process comment:** does the cognitive load map reflect what the humans actually do, or only what the SOP claims they do?
- **At least 1 strength** the participant should preserve and why
- **A one-sentence calibration note:** is this submission tracking toward a Gate 2 pass as currently drafted?

"Everything is fine" reviews are not acceptable — the bar is higher in Week 2 than in Week 1 because you've had one full week of practice giving peer feedback already.
