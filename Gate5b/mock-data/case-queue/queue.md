## Important

- Alert-queue tags ("Standard" / "High") reflect the system's initial categorisation, not a determination. Re-categorise as your analysis demands.
- Some watchlist "hits" are false-positives on common names. Confirm or disconfirm against KYC profile + transaction patterns.
- Some cases sit on the boundary of out-of-scope product lines (remittance). Detect and route accordingly.
- KYC profile data is partially redacted in mock; the system you design must handle full PII appropriately.
