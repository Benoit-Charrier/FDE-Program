COMMERCE CONTROL LIST — CATEGORY 3 EXTRACTS (relevant subset)

[Excerpted for shipment review context. Full CCL at BIS website.]

ECCN 3B991: Semiconductor equipment and components (not specified in 3B001).
  3B991.b: Process-control equipment and parts therefor.
    3B991.b.1: Mask-related equipment.
    3B991.b.1.b: Multi-station process-control subsystems integrating wafer-position metrology with process-parameter control loops.
  License-required for: All destinations except COCOM-cooperating countries.
  License-exception STA: Available for COCOM-cooperating countries with documented end-use and end-user confirmation.
  Reason for control: NS (national security), AT (anti-terrorism).
  BIS guidance note (Oct 2024): Heightened scrutiny on PRC end-users and PRC re-exports through Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam. Recommend extra end-user verification on indirect destinations.

ECCN 3A992: Test, inspection, and production equipment in Category 3.
  3A992.b: Power transformer testing equipment.
  License-required for: Countries in BIS country groups D, E. Generally not required for B (Hong Kong post-2020 reclassified — verify current status).
  License-exception STA: Available for cooperating destinations.
  Reason for control: NS, AT.

ECCN 1C002.b: Specialty alloys.
  1C002.b.1: Nickel-base alloys (Inconel family, including 718, 625, 825).
  1C002.b.2: Cobalt-iron alloys (Hastelloy family).
  License-required for: Wide range; consult country and end-use.
  License-exception STA: Available for cooperating-country destinations with documented end-use.

Note on license-exception STA: STA is available only when (1) consignee is in an eligible country, (2) end-user is documented and consignee certifies compliance, (3) end-use does not involve weapons-of-mass-destruction or military activities.
