# Reference Safety Information — Tezarimab

**Indication:** Treatment of relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis in adults.
**Mechanism:** Anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody; B-cell depletion.
**Dosing:** Initial IV infusion 600 mg, then 600 mg every 6 months.
**Approval date:** 2023-04-18 (FDA), 2023-09-22 (EMA).

## Expected (per Tezarimab RSI v3, current)

**Common (≥1/100):**
- Infusion reactions (occur in ~28% of patients during first infusion; lower in subsequent infusions)
- Upper respiratory tract infections (URTIs)
- Lower respiratory tract infections
- Urinary tract infections
- Headache
- Fatigue
- Decreased immunoglobulin G levels (laboratory finding)
- Decreased neutrophil counts (laboratory finding)

**Uncommon (≥1/1,000 to <1/100):**
- Serious infections requiring hospitalisation, including pneumonia
- Hepatitis B virus reactivation in patients with prior exposure
- Hypersensitivity reactions including delayed-onset (>24 hr post-infusion)
- Decreased serum immunoglobulin levels with associated infection susceptibility

**Rare (≥1/10,000 to <1/1,000):**
- Progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML) — labelled risk
- Severe infusion reactions (anaphylactoid)

**Not currently listed as expected:**
- Hepatic injury / drug-induced liver injury (DILI) of grade 3+ severity in absence of concomitant hepatic risk factors
- Immune thrombocytopenia
- Demyelinating events not consistent with MS course
- Sudden sensorineural hearing loss
