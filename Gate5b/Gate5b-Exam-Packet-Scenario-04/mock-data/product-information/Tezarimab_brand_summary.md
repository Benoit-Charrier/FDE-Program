# Tezarimab — Internal Brand Summary

(Internal Helix reference. For grader / safety physician background.)

**Product label class:** Anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody for relapsing MS.
**Approval status:** FDA 2023-04-18; EMA 2023-09-22; PMDA 2024-06-30; Health Canada 2024-09-11.
**Reference Safety Information current version:** v3, see `Tezarimab_RSI.md`.

**Marketing volume (US 2025):** ~14,000 patients ever-exposed at time of approval; ~22,000 at end of 2024; ~38,000 estimated at year-end 2025.

**Recent safety signals in monitoring:**
- Delayed-onset cytopenia (neutropenia, ITP, combined) — emerging signal, see published case series (`AE-2026-05-14-006_Tezarimab_literature.txt`)
- Severe drug-induced liver injury (Grade 3+ with bilirubin elevation) — one prior expedited filing (HX-AE-25-0322), current case AE-2026-05-09-001 if confirmed will be the second
- Delayed-onset hypersensitivity reactions (>24 hr post-infusion) — listed in RSI v3 as uncommon

**Risk Management Plan commitments:** Quarterly review of cytopenia events; annual immunology cohort analysis.
