# Reference Safety Information — Phaedora

**Indication:** Treatment-resistant major depressive disorder in adults, as adjunctive to standard antidepressant therapy.
**Mechanism:** Selective glutamate receptor modulator (proprietary class).
**Dosing:** Oral, 8 mg daily for first 7 days, titrated to 16 mg daily target dose.
**Approval date:** 2024-08-30 (FDA).

## Expected (per Phaedora RSI v1, current)

**Common (≥1/100):**
- Nausea (typically first 2 weeks)
- Insomnia
- Dizziness
- Dry mouth
- Decreased appetite
- Mild transient blood pressure elevation
- Activation syndrome (irritability, agitation) in first 1-2 weeks at initiation

**Uncommon (≥1/1,000 to <1/100):**
- Tachycardia
- Hyperhidrosis
- Transaminase elevation (typically <3× ULN, resolves)
- Sexual dysfunction (lower frequency than SSRI baseline)

**Rare (≥1/10,000 to <1/1,000):**
- Suicidality in adult patients (boxed warning consistent with antidepressant class)
- Hyponatraemia / SIADH
- Bleeding (in patients on anticoagulants — interaction warning)

**Not currently listed as expected:**
- Serotonin syndrome (Phaedora is not serotonergic per mechanism)
- Severe hypertension requiring hospitalisation
- Stevens-Johnson syndrome / TEN
- Death from any cardiovascular cause
