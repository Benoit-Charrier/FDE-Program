# Reference Safety Information — Solivian

**Indication:** Treatment of chronic kidney disease (Stage 3-4) with proteinuria in adults.
**Mechanism:** Dual endothelin receptor antagonist.
**Dosing:** Oral, 5 mg daily, titrated to 10 mg daily after 4 weeks if tolerated.
**Approval date:** 2021-11-12 (FDA).

## Expected (per Solivian RSI v4, current)

**Common (≥1/100):**
- Fluid retention / oedema (peripheral, ankle)
- Headache
- Anaemia (mild)
- Transient blood pressure decrease
- Nasal congestion
- Decreased haemoglobin (laboratory finding)
- Mild transaminase elevation (typically <2× ULN)

**Uncommon (≥1/1,000 to <1/100):**
- Heart failure exacerbation in patients with prior history
- Severe hypotension
- Hepatic injury (transaminases >3× ULN with bilirubin elevation)

**Rare (≥1/10,000 to <1/1,000):**
- Drug-induced liver injury with hepatocellular pattern (boxed warning)
- Embryo-foetal toxicity (boxed warning, contraindication in pregnancy)
- Acute kidney injury / AKI

**Not currently listed as expected:**
- Pancreatitis
- Stevens-Johnson syndrome / TEN
- Sudden cardiac death
