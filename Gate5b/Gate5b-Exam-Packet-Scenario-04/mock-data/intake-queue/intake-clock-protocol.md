# Helix PV Intake-Clock Protocol (Internal Reference)

Helix Therapeutics operates a Day-0 receipt rule consistent with ICH E2D and FDA guidance:

- **Day 0** = calendar day on which any Helix employee, contractor, or vendor first receives the AE information, regardless of whether intake formally opens the case.
- This includes:
  - Helix medical information line receiving a phone call
  - Helix sales representative receiving a verbal report during a clinic visit
  - Helix-contracted social-media monitoring vendor surfacing a post
  - Helix-contracted clinical-trial site investigator reporting via the trial's safety reporting pathway
  - Literature alerts surfaced by Helix's contracted literature surveillance vendor
- **Day 15** is calendar day 15 from Day 0 for serious-unexpected reportable events to FDA. Filing by 23:59 ET on Day 15.
- A minimally identifiable patient + reporter + suspect drug + AE is required for an ICSR (Individual Case Safety Report). Lacking any of these four, the case is not a valid ICSR and the clock does not advance pending follow-up — but Helix's documented follow-up attempts must be defensible.
